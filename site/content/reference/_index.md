The Reference explains every feature, key and setting of textual-items++, one topic to a page. The key and settings tables come straight from the app's source, from its command registry, its default keymap and its settings window, so they show exactly what the app does.

A few conventions run through every page:

- **Keys** are written the way the app writes them in its panels. `Ctrl+B` means hold Ctrl and press B. `gt` and `Ctrl+W v` are sequences: press one key after the other, within a second. A capital letter, such as `G`, means Shift with that letter.
- **Several keys** in one row are alternatives: any of them runs the command.
- **No key** means the command is reached from the [command palette](@/reference/finders.md#key-command-palette) or the context menu instead.
- **Links.** Every command, window and setting row has an anchor of its own, so you can link to it.

In the app, press `?` to see the keys for where you are, and `g?` to open the [keymap](@/reference/keyboard.md#key-keymap), which lists every command at once. `Ctrl+P` opens the command palette with every command by name. For every key on one page, print the [cheat sheet](@/reference/cheatsheet.md).
