A description is for a line or two. For longer notes, a checklist or a running log of comments, every item has **attachments**, kept in a panel on the right.

## Open the panel

Open the Customer newsletter project from the pinned group in the sidebar. The focus is on "Write the welcome issue", whose row shows `0/3`: a checklist with three items, none done yet. Press `\`.

The attachments panel opens on the right with three parts: the task's **note**, its **checklist** and its **comments**. The status line says `ATTACHMENTS`, because the panel now has the keys.

## Work in the panel

Press `j` to move down to "Intro from the founders" and press `x`: it's ticked off, and the row behind the panel now says `1/3`. Checklist items work like tasks: `e` or `Enter` edits the one you're on.

Now press `c` to add a comment. It's stamped with the current date and time. Type `Ana will send the founders' intro on Tuesday` and press `Enter`.

Press `\` again and the panel takes the whole width of the pane, handy for a long note. One more `\` hides it. `Esc` closes it from either size.

Projects and areas have attachments too. On the project's title at the top, or anywhere in it, press `g` `\` to open the panel for the project itself: you'll find its goals in the note and two earlier comments.

## Comment without opening anything

To jot a quick remark, press `c` right in the list. Go to the Inbox, press `c` on "Call the vet about Pepper's vaccinations", type `Pepper's last shot was in March` and press `Enter`. The comment is added and a comment icon appears on the row, without the panel ever opening.

The [Reference](@/reference/attachments.md) covers the panel's keys and how attachments are stored in the file.
