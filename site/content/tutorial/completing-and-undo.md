## Check a task off

In the Inbox, move to "Buy a birthday card for Grandma" and press `x` (or `.`). The box gets a tick and the task disappears from the list: completed tasks are hidden so the list only shows what's left to do.

They're not gone. Press `Ctrl+O` to show completed items. They gather at the bottom under **Completed Items**, most recent first.

{{ shot(src="tutorial/completed", alt="The Inbox with three open tasks and, below a Completed Items heading, Buy a birthday card for Grandma with a tick", caption="Ctrl+O shows the completed items under the open ones.") }}

Press `Ctrl+O` again to hide them.

## The Logbook

Press `g` `l` for the **Logbook**. It collects every completed item from the whole file, newest first, grouped by the day you finished it, each with the place it lives.

{{ shot(src="tutorial/logbook", alt="The Logbook with Buy a birthday card for Grandma under Today, then Pick an email tool and Write the job post under earlier days, each followed by its area and project", caption="The Logbook groups finished work by day.") }}

The Logbook moves nothing. Completing a task leaves it exactly where it was in the file, with a `@done` tag and the date.

## Undo and redo

Go back to the Inbox with `g` `i` and press `u` (or `z`). The completion is undone and the birthday card is back. `Z` redoes what you undid. Every change can be undone, a moved project or a deleted section just as well as a typo. Try `Z` now to complete the card again, then `u` once more: you'll need the card open in the next chapter.

## The Trash

Move to "Look into a standing desk" and press `d` `d`. The task goes to the **Trash**. Press `g` `d` to see it there. To bring it back, press `R` on it: it returns to exactly where it was. Do that now, then go back to the Inbox with `g` `i`.

Nothing in the Trash is deleted for good until you empty it with `d` `e` there.

More on completing, the Logbook and the Trash is in the [Reference](@/reference/tasks.md).
