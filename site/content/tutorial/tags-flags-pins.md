## Tag a task

Tags like `@errands` or `@calls` cut across your areas: everything to do in town, everyone to phone. In the Inbox, move to "Look into a standing desk" and press `m` `g`. The tag picker lists the tags the file already uses. Type `office`: there's no such tag yet, so the picker offers to make one.

{{ shot(src="tutorial/tag-picker", alt="The Tags picker over the Inbox with office typed and a single entry, office, marked new tag", caption="Typing a name that doesn't exist yet makes a new tag.") }}

Press `Space` to put the tag on, then `Enter` to close the picker: `@office` now shows after the title. `Space` on a tag the task already has takes it off, and you can toggle several before closing.

## Filter by tags

Press `g` `a` for **All**, every open task in the file grouped by where it lives. That's a long list. Press `g` `f`, type `errands`, press `Space` and then `Enter`. Now only the errands are left, from the Inbox and from Home alike, and the tag shows at the top right of the pane.

{{ shot(src="tutorial/tag-filter", alt="The All view filtered to @errands, showing Buy a birthday card for Grandma and Return the library books from the Inbox and Replace the hallway light bulb from Home", caption="All, filtered to errands: a shopping list from the whole file.") }}

The filter works on any list, a project or Today just as well. Press `g` `F` to clear it.

## Flags

A flag marks a task or a project as important. Go to the Home area, move to the "Plan Grandma's 90th" project and press `m` `f`. A red flag appears in front of its title, here and in the sidebar.

Press `g` `!` for **Flagged**, which gathers every flagged project and task in the file. "Write the welcome issue" was flagged already in the sample file.

## Pins

Projects you open every day deserve a place at the top of the sidebar. Go back with `H`, move to "Weekend in Edinburgh" and press `m` `p`. The project now also shows in the pinned group under the views, next to Customer newsletter. Press `m` `p` again on it to unpin it.

{{ shot(src="tutorial/flagged", alt="The Flagged view listing the flagged Plan Grandma's 90th project and the Write the welcome issue task; the sidebar shows Weekend in Edinburgh pinned under Customer newsletter", caption="Flagged, with a newly pinned project in the sidebar.") }}
