## Up and down

Press `j` to move down and `k` to move up, or use the arrow keys. `G` jumps to the last item and `g` `g` back to the top. Keys written side by side like that are pressed one after the other, not together.

## The sidebar

Press `Tab`. The focus moves to the sidebar, onto the Inbox. Now press `j` a few times: the pane follows along, showing Today, then Upcoming, then each view in turn. Keep going until you reach **WORK**. The pane now shows the Work area: its description, its own tasks, and its two projects.

Press `Tab` again to put the focus back in the pane, on "Submit the expense report".

## Open a project

Press `j` twice to land on the "Customer newsletter" row.

Press `Enter`. The project opens in the pane: its description, its tasks, and its Launch section at the bottom. `Enter` opens whatever you're on, a project or an area. On a task it opens the edit dialog instead, which the next chapter covers.

To go back, press `H` (or `Alt+←`). You're in the Work area again, on the row you left. `L` goes forward again, like a browser.

`g` `i` takes you straight to the Inbox from anywhere.

## Make room

Press `Ctrl+B` (or `|`). The focus jumps to the sidebar, onto whatever the pane shows. Press it again and the sidebar disappears, giving the pane the whole width. A third press brings it back.

That's all you need to get anywhere. The [Reference](@/reference/navigation.md) lists the rest of the motions.
