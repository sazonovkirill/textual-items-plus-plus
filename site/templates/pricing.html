The fine print, honestly

Is textual-items++ really free?
Yes. Every feature above is in the one and only version, and all three plans are the same app.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no account, no sign-in, no subscription and no telemetry. The app reads and writes one file on your disk, and that's it.
What's the license?
It is open source under the MIT license.
How do I get it?
With Rust installed, run {{ config.extra.install }}, then tipp todo.taskpaper. The Tutorial takes it from there.
